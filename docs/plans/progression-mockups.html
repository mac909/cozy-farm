Restoring the village

Mockups for progression.md, all at iPhone size (390 × 844 CSS px, the notch and home bar drawn in). The market is drawn from the real map and sprites; the board, the lighthouse and the materials are placeholders until they're drawn in tools/sprites/. Click a phone to see it at full size. Names (Pip, Juno, Marlow) are stand-ins.

Marlow, the board, and giving

The board stands right of the seed stand, on the path everyone already walks. Holding something a project needs, the Action button says Give; otherwise Board, which opens the card. Faces show who helped; never counts.

Celebrations

The last thing in cheers on both screens at once. The work happens overnight, so the morning shows what changed. A finished project gets its own card; the lighthouse waits for dusk.

You, and your home

Nothing new on the HUD: your face (top right) opens the You card, with a gold dot when something's new there. Level-ups show only on your own screen. House upgrades are given at the board's second tab, by either of you.

The finished village

The map grows 32 columns east (48 → 80), so nothing saved moves. Today's village is drawn from the real map; the new land to the right is the plan. Colours say which milestone builds each place.
