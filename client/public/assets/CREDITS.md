  - Sounds: menu accept/cancel/move, coin, water, grass, bubble, waves, a success jingle

## Our own sprites

- **Folder:** `custom/`
- Made for Cozy Farm in Ninja Adventure's style, for things the pack doesn't have: crop growth stages, harvested crops and seed packets, tilled and watered soil, beachcombing finds (shells, sea glass, a starfish), the shipping crate, the seed stand, beach sand, a wave emote, music on/off, arrange/turn/done, book and stop icons, the map's marker tiles, seaside furniture (a life ring on a post, an anchor, a beach umbrella, a ship in a bottle, a fish tank), twelve fish, a bobber and a firefly; and for the village (Milestone 6): the lighthouse in four states, the Village Board, brambles, driftwood, a branch and three night fish; and for Milestone 7: a porch (decking, a bench, a lantern dark and lit), six level gifts (a straw hat on a stand, a mounted fish, a shell wreath, a golden watering-can planter, a lobster pot, a butterfly jar) and golden crops (our own crop pictures recoloured in gold); and for Milestone 8: ten more crops (growth stages, harvested item, seed packet), five tool upgrades, the garden shop in four states, autumn round trees and the grass in autumn and winter.
- `bugs.png` also holds the pack's two butterflies and three recolours of them (CC0), and `fishing.png` the pack's fishing rod on a 16×16 frame.
- Milestone 8's recolours of the pack (CC0): `garden-shop.png` is the pack's little shop with the sign (TilesetHouse) given a green roof, our own sign, flower boxes, boards and a potting bench; `tools.png`'s big watering can, wide hoe and good rod are the pack's can, hoe and rod in copper, brass and red lacquer (the net and the basket are ours); `seasonal-trees.png` is the pack's two green round trees (TilesetNature) in autumn colours; `beach-terrain-autumn.png` and `beach-terrain-winter.png` are our beach terrain with the grass recoloured.
- The beach sand sheets are recolours of the pack's own floor and water autotiles (CC0), so their edges match the pack exactly. The emote sheet reuses the pack's speech bubble and heart.
- They're drawn as data in `tools/sprites/` and rendered with `npm run sprites`. Edit the data and re-render rather than editing the PNGs.
